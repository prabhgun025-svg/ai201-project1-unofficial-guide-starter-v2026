THREAD: Commuting an hour each way — is it survivable?


Yes, but stack your courses. Three long days beats five short ones by a wide margin.
The commuter lounge in the Campus Center Basement has lockers you can rent for a semester (completely free)
Watch the evening bus timetable before you register for anything that ends after 6pm, if you live nearby, it should be fine
I commuted for two years. The thing that made it work was treating the train as study time rather than dead time.
