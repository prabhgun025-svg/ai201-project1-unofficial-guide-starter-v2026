THREAD: Best study spots that aren't the library?

Campus Center Commuter Lounges on the second floor is a good study spot (tho fairly noisy)
Tech Cafe is good if you like the cafe aesthetic. 
newark has a lot of cafes so going to any one of them is fine for studying 
Any empty classroom is fine to study and work in. I conduct meetings in there sometimes. 
The academic buildings (such as GITC, CKB, etc) have a lot of study spots that are fairly quiet. CKB also has breakout rooms if needed (tho it is 2 or more people)
