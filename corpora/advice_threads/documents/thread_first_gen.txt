THREAD: Anything specific for first-generation students?

Always utilize the advising office for any academic needs such as course registration and what not. Utilize all and every office that you believe may benefit you 
This includes, financial aid and bursar office for tuition, scholarship, money
CCAPS or Peer Wellness for peer support 
Peer Wellness hosts First Gen talking events if you need anything such as friends or support as well 
Attend events for free food and stuff and to make friends as well
