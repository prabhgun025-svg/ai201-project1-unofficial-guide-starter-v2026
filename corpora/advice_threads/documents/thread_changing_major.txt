THREAD: How hard is it to change major in second year?

Administratively trivial - it's a form. The real question is whether the credits you've taken map onto the new requirements.
This depends enormously on the direction. This mostly depends on the major. 
Talk to the department adviser for the major you want, not your current one. They know the exceptions.
For example --  switching from Information technology to Computer Science will require an extra semester more often than not due to the math and science requirements 
But computer science to information technology is easier since all of the CS requirements can fit nicely into the IT requirements (IT does not require math and science as heavily)
This goes for any major
